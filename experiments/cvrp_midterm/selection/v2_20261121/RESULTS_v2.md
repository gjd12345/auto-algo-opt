## Design

Three binary factors crossed, demand distribution held fixed at U{1, 9}: spatial = uniform/clustered, depot = centre/eccentric, capacity = loose/tight. 12 instances per cell, 50 customers, 96 instances total.

Capacity levels are fixed integers: {'loose': 45, 'tight': 28}. Capacity ratio (total demand / capacity) therefore varies with the demand draw but stays near 5.5 under `loose` and 9.0 under `tight`.

- every saving is exactly zero while standing at the depot: True
- tie-break at the depot: larger d(0, j) wins
- is full Clarke-Wright: False
